# TMSG corporate rebuild - reference decisions

The design uses original TMSG content, branding and illustrative imagery. It adapts information architecture and interaction patterns, not third-party brand assets or copied page content.

## References reviewed

- Mahindra - https://www.mahindra.com/: clear Who We Are / What We Do navigation, direct business actions, visible sector discovery and distinct people/purpose sections. Homepage and sector-grid layouts were visually inspected.
- Tata - https://www.tata.com/: editorial lead stories, brand discovery and separate careers content. Homepage and editorial section were visually inspected.
- Brookfield - https://www.brookfield.com/: clear business/capability navigation, an explicit approach, audience-led partnership routes and structured perspectives. Homepage was visually inspected.
- Virgin - https://www.virgin.com/: the homepage was blocked with a CloudFront 403 in this browser. Official search-indexed brand guidance at https://www.virgin.com/about-virgin/latest/making-our-mark informed the principle of distinct business identities connected by a group purpose; its current live homepage was not claimed as inspected.

## Implemented structure

1. Corporate header: utility links; group, sector and commitment mega menus; searchable page directory; accessible mobile navigation.
2. Homepage lead: a clear description of TMSG, three editable group/business highlights, direct business links and motion controls.
3. Business discovery: a single index showing the sector, business name, specific focus and child-site link for all five destinations.
4. Group approach: visible explanations of the four development stages, without hiding single-sentence content behind tabs.
5. Responsibilities: specific priorities around environment, people and integrity rather than unsupported impact metrics.
6. Group briefs: four source-based introductions to the group, business concepts and sustainability priorities. No fabricated dated company announcements.
7. Careers and partnerships: clear separate visitor paths.
8. Child websites: five connected destinations, each with five routes, sector-specific messaging, branding and contact handling.

## Approved colour direction

Midnight navy (#101D35) replaces green throughout the group website and all five business destinations, including image overlays, menus, buttons, forms, footers and the 404 page. Warm ivory (#F6F3ED) and white keep content sections light; champagne gold (#C3A46B) highlights actions and fine details; charcoal (#263244) is the body-text colour. A darker gold tone is used where small text needs greater contrast against ivory or white. Child-site layouts, typography, content, motion and the supplied crest are retained.

## Editorial component review

The latest review was made against the source code and the supplied TMSG content PDF. It identifies presentation patterns that felt generic; it does not claim to infer an image’s provenance from its appearance.

| Previous component | Rebuilt component |
| --- | --- |
| Five large sector-image tiles followed by a second business directory | One business index with sector, brand, specific focus and a child-site link |
| Repeated full-width stock-photo banners on corporate and child subpages | Text-led mastheads with page titles, descriptions and real breadcrumb links |
| Oversized stage numbers and tabs that hid a single sentence | Visible business approach and principle lists |
| Stock team photo, decorative partnership circles and generic promotional slogans | Separate partnership and career routes explaining what to submit |
| Founder name poster with ornamental keyword badges | A founder profile and the chairman’s quotation verified against page 5 of the supplied PDF |
| Numbered child-business focus cards and a repeated collaboration-photo panel | Focus indexes linking to the relevant details and a concise group affiliation section |
| Generic article cards and region tiles | Source-based group/business briefs and a clear list of future market priorities |
| Stock imagery inside the mega menus | Contextual founder, healthcare and sustainability information |

The supplied crest, sector-specific landing-page imagery, navy/ivory/champagne palette, genuine motion controls and enquiry storage are retained. An Omviraa CTA still referring to the removed green button class was corrected to navy. No employee portraits, projects, operating statistics or current vacancies were invented.

## Interior refresh from supplied screenshots

The four screenshots showed oversized text-only headings, repeated three-column sections and long stretches of empty space. The refresh replaces these with photographic covers, a purpose/vision/mission tab set, a visual portfolio, an interactive values composition and a photographed development process. Leadership uses the supplied crest and verified quotation, without inventing a portrait. Business directory, market outlook, sustainability, careers, partnership and contact pages each have their own composition. The five business destinations and their story, focus, approach and contact routes share a coherent system while retaining sector-specific typography and imagery. The footer is condensed into three navigation groups. Photographs remain labelled as illustrative.

## Verification

All 42 routes render with one main heading. Internal pages, fragment links, images, search and enquiry handling pass the automated checks. Browser review covered desktop renderings and phone/tablet layouts, with no horizontal overflow in the responsive samples. Purpose tabs, values, development stages, sector filters, region selection, partnership options and the mobile dialog were exercised in the browser. The earlier preview restriction was resolved for this review. Live production enquiry records were not created for testing. Source content, search labels and page titles were scanned for literal and encoded em dashes and use normal hyphens.
